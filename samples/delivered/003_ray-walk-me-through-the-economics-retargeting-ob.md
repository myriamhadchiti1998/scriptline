## Hooks

**Hook 1 — pattern interrupt · direct challenge**

Don't take my word for it. Look at who pays me. Then decide.

**Hook 2 — alternate angle · contrarian admission**

There is a catch. I'd rather tell you now than have you find it later.

**Hook 3 — third option · unexpected specific**

Eleven signed placement agreements. The liquidators behind them pay me. Not you.

## Main body

**PAIN**

You've seen the page. You've read the fine print. Now you want the economics. Who pays this guy, and what happens to him if my store just sits there? What's the catch this time? That's the right question. I'll walk through it in order, and I'll name the one real catch before I'm done.

**PROMISE**

Here's the chain. Regional liquidators always have overstock and returned inventory to clear. They pay a placement fee to move it through small storefronts. I only get that fee when a member's store actually moves their inventory. I'm not paid up front for results. If your store doesn't move product, I don't get paid. My income depends on your store working. Then the match sheet: a weekly list of lots the partners most need to clear, matched to store types. So you start with inventory that already has demand behind it. And the work is a few evenings a week, with a cataloguing system and templates for listing, pricing, and returns. I won't insult you by calling it passive.

**PROOF**

Now check it. I managed a regional wholesale logistics depot for 14 years. I have 11 vetted regional liquidator partners under signed placement agreements. The program's been running 5 years, with weekly match sheets that whole time. There are 9,400+ enrolled members across the US, Canada, the UK, and Australia. And I won't give you an income number, because anyone who does shouldn't be trusted. Ray C., an analyst in Canada, said: "I went in looking for the catch. The catch is that you have to actually do the weekly work. The economics hold up, which is more than I can say for the last three." That's the catch I promised. The weekly work is on you.

**CTA**

So here's the next step. See this week's matched inventory. The lots refresh weekly, so what you see is current. Or if you'd rather watch the whole mechanism first, the First Pallet workshop is a dollar. Two hours, live, end to end. Watch it, then decide.
