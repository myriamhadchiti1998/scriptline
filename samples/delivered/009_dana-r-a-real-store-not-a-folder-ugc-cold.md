## Main body

**PAIN**

Maybe there's a course sitting in a folder somewhere, half watched, and you'd rather not open it. You saw the red flags and wanted it to work anyway. Now every online store pitch gets the same question: what's the catch this time? And you've stopped trusting your own judgment. Dana R. was sure this was another version of the thing that burned her. Then one moment changed that. I'll tell you what it was.

**PROMISE**

Here's the reframe. A program that gets paid the moment you buy has no reason to care whether your store works. This one runs the other way. Regional liquidators have overstock and returned inventory to clear. They pay me a placement fee only when a member's store actually moves it, so my income depends on your store working. Every week there's a match sheet: lots the partners most need to clear, matched to store types. The demand exists before your store does. The work is a few evenings a week, with templates for listing, pricing, and returns. I won't call that passive.

**PROOF**

Check it, because you should. I managed a regional wholesale logistics depot for 14 years. I hold signed placement agreements with 11 vetted regional liquidator partners. The program's been running 5 years, with weekly match sheets that whole time. There are 9,400+ enrolled members across the US, Canada, the UK, and Australia. I won't give you an income number, because anyone who does shouldn't be trusted. Dana R., an office admin in Ohio, said: "I was sure this was another version of the thing that cost me $2,400. Then he explained who actually pays him and it clicked." That's the moment I promised. Who pays me: the liquidators, and only when a member's store moves product.

**CTA**

If you want to see it before you decide anything, watch the First Pallet workshop for $1. See exactly how it works, then decide. It's two hours, live, and it walks the full mechanism end to end, with a sample match sheet to look at. The link's below.
