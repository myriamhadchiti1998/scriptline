## Hooks

## Main body

**PAIN**

If you've been burned, you know the feeling. You saw the red flags and wanted it to work anyway. Maybe you never finished the last one, and now you don't trust your own judgment. So you ask: what's the catch this time? Good. I'll tell you the one real catch before I'm done.

**PROMISE**

Here's the question that matters: who actually pays me? The liquidators do. Regional liquidators always have overstock and returned inventory they need to clear. They pay me a placement fee only when a member's store actually moves it. Members don't pay me up front for results, so my income depends on your store working. Then there's the match sheet, refreshed weekly: lots the partners most need to clear, matched to store types. The demand exists before your store does. The work is a few evenings a week, with templates for listing, pricing, and returns. I won't insult you by calling it passive.

**PROOF**

Now check it, because you should. I managed a regional wholesale logistics depot for 14 years. I hold signed placement agreements with 11 vetted regional liquidator partners. The program's been running five years, with weekly match sheets that whole time. There are 9,400+ enrolled members across the US, Canada, the UK, and Australia. I won't give you an income number, because anyone who does shouldn't be trusted. Dana R., an office admin in Ohio, says: "Then he explained who actually pays him and it clicked. I have a real store now, not a folder of unwatched videos." And Ray C., an analyst in Canada, says: "I went in looking for the catch. The catch is that you have to actually do the weekly work." That's the catch I promised, and it's a fair one. The weekly work is yours.

**CTA**

If you want to see it for yourself, watch the First Pallet workshop for $1. It's two hours, live, and it walks the full mechanism end to end. You'll get a sample match sheet to inspect too. See exactly how it works, then decide.
