## Main body

**PAIN**

If you've been burned before, let me guess how it went. You bought the course. Then life got in the way, and you never finished it. Now every new thing makes you think, what's the catch this time? And you don't trust your own judgment, because it already cost you once. So you've decided the problem is you. I don't think it is. There's one question I'd ask before handing over a card, and I'll give it to you in a minute.

**PROMISE**

Here's the difference. Regional liquidators always have overstock and returned inventory to clear. They pay a placement fee to move it through small, reliable storefronts. When a member's store moves that inventory, the liquidator pays me. Members don't pay me up front for results. If your store doesn't work, my income doesn't either. Every week, members get a match sheet: the lots the partners most need to clear, matched to store types. So the demand exists before your store does. Your part is a few evenings a week. I won't call that passive.

**PROOF**

Now the question. Who's actually writing the check? Ask it of anyone, me included. The liquidators write mine, and only when a store moves product. I spent 14 years managing a regional wholesale logistics depot. I have 11 vetted regional liquidator partners under signed placement agreements. The program's been running 5 years, with weekly match sheets the whole time, and 9,400+ members across the US, Canada, the UK and Australia. I won't give you a number, because anyone who does shouldn't be trusted. Dana R., a member in Ohio, was burned by a dropshipping course. She said, "Then he explained who actually pays him and it clicked. I have a real store now, not a folder of unwatched videos."

**CTA**

If you want to see it before you decide anything, watch the First Pallet workshop for $1. It's two hours, live, and it walks the full mechanism end to end. You also get a sample match sheet to look at. See exactly how it works, then decide. The link's below.
