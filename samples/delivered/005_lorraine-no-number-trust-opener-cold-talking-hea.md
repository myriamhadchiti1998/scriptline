## Hooks

**Hook 1 — pattern interrupt · private thought callout**

Will the savings stretch? You've done that sum on the back of an envelope more than once. I'm not going to promise you a number. That's the point.

**Hook 2 — alternate angle · unexpected specific**

The core of my 9,400+ members skews 45 to 55. If you're wondering whether this is for someone your age, that's who it's built for.

**Hook 3 — third option · implicating question**

Be honest: how many pitches have promised you a fortune without asking a single question? I'm going to do the opposite.

## Main body

**PAIN**

Here's what I think is going on. You're watching the savings, and every pitch you get is about a fortune. But you don't need a fortune. You need steady. A small, real project you could be proud of. And you're wondering, can someone like me actually do this, when I'm no computer person? Those pitches never answer that. I'll tell you why I won't give you a number, and it isn't caution. I'll get to it.

**PROMISE**

Here's the reframe. The question isn't how much. It's who pays, and when. Regional liquidators always have overstock and returned inventory they need to clear. They pay me a placement fee, but only when a member's store actually moves their stock. Members don't pay me up front for results. So my income depends on your store working. Every week there's a match sheet: lots the partners most need to clear, matched to store types, so you start with inventory that already has demand behind it. Your part is a few evenings a week, with templates for listing, pricing and returns. I won't call that passive.

**PROOF**

So, why no number. Because a number from me would be a guess about a store I can't see, and anyone who gives you one shouldn't be trusted. Structure, you can check. I managed a regional wholesale logistics depot for 14 years. I have 11 vetted regional liquidator partners under signed placement agreements. The program's been running 5 years, with weekly match sheets that whole time. There are 9,400+ members across the US, Canada, the UK and Australia. Lorraine P., semi-retired in England, said: "The fact he never promised me money is exactly why I trusted him."

**CTA**

If you'd like to see it before deciding anything: watch the First Pallet workshop for $1. See exactly how it works, then decide. It's two hours, live, with a sample match sheet to look at. Or grab a $1 seat. The link's below.
